Performance notes
Generic Insights:-
1. Spend rose but orders didn’t. 
- Sessions were up 12% and conversion fell 10%
- Partially due to AB Test, which takes up around $600 

2. The slide started in the last three days. 
- 29–31 Oct had an MER of only 6.15x, against 9.3x–10.9x in weeks 1–4. 

3. Existing customers were 266 of 579 orders (46%), down 3%.New customers were up 4%.

Creative Insights:-
1. Upfront-product-hook is the workhorse and it hooks worst.
- 44.5% of video spend, 7.01x against a 6.55x average, at an 11.6% hook rate
- Showing the product immediately filters the audience: fewer people stop, and the ones who do are closer to buying.

2. Good hook rate doesn't mean good ROAS.
