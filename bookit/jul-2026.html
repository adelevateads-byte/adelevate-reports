Insights & recommendations · July 2026
Insights
1
Cost efficiency
Cost per lead moved to RM11.71 this month, while cost per subscriber moved to RM62.39, a wider swing. Conversion slipped to 18.8% from 21.1%, which is the main factor here, more than the cost of the leads themselves.
2
Lead channel mix
WhatsApp Messaging remained the main driver this month, contributing 229 leads, 67% of the total. It was also this month's biggest mover, down 84, so even the dominant channel pulled back this month, worth checking whether that was intentional.
3
Conversion funnel health
18.8% of leads became subscribers this month, down 2.4 points from 21.1%, while lead volume fell 19%. The dip sits in the conversion step rather than lead generation, worth a closer look before pushing more spend into the top of the funnel.
4
Spend concentration
Prospecting | Sales Messaging | WA | In-House Content accounted for 27% of this month's spend, with a cost per lead running 20% below the account average. As the largest campaign in the account, it's also doing the most to keep overall costs down.
Recommendations
1
Budget allocation
Prospecting | Sales Messaging | WA | Agency Content produced the cheapest leads this month at RM8.75, compared to RM43.86 for Prospecting | Website Conversion | Sales, a meaningful gap between two campaigns on the same platform. We'd recommend shifting some budget from the latter into the former next month and watching whether the cost advantage holds as spend increases.
2
Lead engagement quality
47.3% of the leads reached this month replied back the same day, up from 44.4% last month, a sign the messaging and audience are resonating well. Worth keeping the current creative and targeting approach in place as volume grows.
3
Creative refresh vs scale
NewSeriesPost-5 was this month's strongest performer, with 68 leads at RM7.86 each. 8 of the account's 23 active creatives are each spending under 2% of budget with little to show for it, we'd recommend shifting that spend toward the top performers and retiring or refreshing the underperforming 8.
4
Video content format
Only 46% of 3-second plays reach the 25% watched mark, the steepest drop in the funnel, and just 8% watch to the end. Since most of the loss happens right at the start, we'd recommend prioritising a stronger opening hook on new creative over changes to the middle or end of the video.
