Strategy note Your own thoughts, saved when you download for client

What we've implemented this period

  • Restructured the account into two campaign types — always-on/evergreen (BAU) kept separate from tactical, thematic sales pushes, so ongoing performance and promo-specific results don't get blended together.
  • Split targeting into two distinct ad sets — genuine cold prospecting and a separate warm retargeting layer — directly addressing the funnel gap flagged in the original audit.
  • UTM tagging is now applied on all new campaigns, so orders can be tracked back to the specific ad that drove them.
  • Refined audience segments with a 30-day retention window, plus new custom audiences built from more precise signals.
  • Set up shared post IDs so the same creative accumulates engagement instead of fragmenting across duplicates.
  • Daily reporting is now live, giving more real-time visibility than the previous monthly cadence.

A deliberate note on what you'll see in Ads Manager
Old and new campaign structures are running side by side during this transition, which naturally invites a direct comparison. We'd caution against reading too much into that early on — creative differences alone can explain a gap, separate from the structural change. That's also why new and old campaigns are intentionally running different visuals for now, to keep the read clean while the new structure stabilises. A proper side-by-side A/B test is on the table later, once performance settles.

Next steps

  1. Current phase: stabilise the fundamentals — structure, tracking, audiences.
  2. Next: content funnelling (matching creative to the right funnel stage) and structured creative/A-B testing.
  3. Later: a full CRO pass on the site, once the website revamp is complete.

Pending Discussions

  1. Confirmation on breakeven MER and target MER.
  2. Hybrid between omnichannel and multichannel strategy.
  3. Campaign planning on tactical/sales campaigns.
